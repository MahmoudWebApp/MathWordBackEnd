## Example evidence tasks (not importable items)

- Grade 1 measurement: compare objects spanning 4 and 6 equal blocks; shorter by 2 blocks. Also use time and indirect comparison tasks.
- Grade 1 geometry: classify a triangle rotated in a picture by its three sides, not color or orientation. Requires an accessible diagram or equivalent text.
- Grade 1 data: three groups contain 4, 2, 5 items; total 11, largest group 5. Ask one independently assessed objective per item.
- Grade 2 geometry: two rectangles cut into four equal shares; recognize quarters regardless of orientation.
- Grade 2 data: a single-unit bar chart records 7 red and 4 blue counters; difference 3. Provide accessible chart representation.
- Grade 6 statistics: data 2, 3, 3, 4, 18 have median 3 and mean 6; explain which better describes a typical observation in context.
- Grade 6 signed numbers: order -3, -1/2, 0, 2; recognize negative magnitude is not numeric order.

## Publication gates

1. Recheck existing primary skills and all draft/live stems, including the 22-draft placement audit; reuse matching skills rather than create near-synonyms.
2. Review Cambridge progression and exact CCSS standard codes at objective level; maintain Arabic/English parity and original student history.
3. Draft genuinely distinct learning tasks, four distinct options, a unique correct answer, hints, formula/idea and verified solution. A count of 20 alone is not adequate evidence of coverage.
4. Validate category/skill/track ownership and Free/Premium effects through the existing backend preview and editorial workflow. No direct production insert, empty category publication, history rewrite or entitlement change.
5. Only after reviewed content exists, create missing skills/links and publish via the authorized workflow. Re-audit the live five-category/twenty-question coverage and monitor database health.

University gaps (26 category slots) need an independent course-module review against CUPM and MSC2020. Do not assign unrelated academic branches to a course or split a coherent subject solely to achieve five categories.
