## Adaptive production pacing
Use a single serial writer. The client increases its delay from 200 ms on slow responses (>=2 s), decreases it gradually on healthy responses, and stops after three consecutive slow responses or one response >=8 s. The confirmed publication journal is recorded before a pressure stop. No automatic retry on 429, 503, timeout or ambiguous mutation. Re-preview and check actual DB state before resuming. These are conservative initial client latency thresholds, not direct Disk IO telemetry; monitor Supabase Disk IO and pool health separately. Packing is linear in candidate count (one serialization per row). Generation/verification should remain offline. Do not claim support for thousands of pedagogically distinct questions until new families are actually authored and reviewed.
